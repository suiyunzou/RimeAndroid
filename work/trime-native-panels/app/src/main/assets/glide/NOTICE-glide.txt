The bundled glide lexicons derive from the already deployed ReRime Wanxiang public
library release wanxiang-precompiled-50-880ac18dd08e, dictionary contributors amzxyz
and rime-wanxiang contributors, under CC BY 4.0. The assets/glide directory retains
the upstream license, NOTICE, reproducible extraction recipe and per-file hashes.
Changes: remove Pinyin tone marks, use v for ü, retain high-weight entries and
exclude non-letter codes; do not copy any user dictionary or learned data.

The decoder independently implements normalized point geometry, endpoint filtering
and bounded dynamic time warping. It uses neither ReRime's proprietary swipe model
nor any recorded human swipe training corpus. It recognizes dictionary-backed
English words and Chinese Pinyin trajectories; arbitrary sentences and indistinct
traces are not guaranteed. Candidate confirmation is required before committing.
